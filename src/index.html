Taildesk 0.2.0

A standalone Windows VPN client for your existing Tailscale network. The official desktop app is not required.

Includes its own networking engine, Windows service, adapter, and browser sign-in. View devices, copy addresses, check reachability, reconnect, choose an exit node, and manage connection settings.

Based on Tailscale’s open-source networking engine. Your existing Tailscale account and coordination service are still used. Taildesk is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tailscale. Third-party licenses are included with the application.

Taildesk joins as a separate device. Account switching and file-transfer controls are not included in this version. Closing this window keeps the interface in the tray; enable Run unattended to keep the VPN connected after quitting or signing out. Use Disconnect or Stop service to end the connection.
